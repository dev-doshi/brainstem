First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric, server-side).

Exact-equivalence optimisations: same results and the same random draws as vanilla, with less work.
- Zombie village goal (MoveThroughVillageGoal): skips searches that provably can't reach a village.
- Brain behaviours: rejects behaviours whose required memories are missing, without running them.
- Job-site competitor scan: an indexed lookup instead of scanning nearby villagers.
- TemptGoal: skips players who are provably out of range before the held-item check.

Every module has a built-in verify mode (config/brainstem.json: "verify": "sample" or "all") that checks results against vanilla and switches a module off on any difference.
Not compatible with Async.
